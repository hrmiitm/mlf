MLF STUDY ROOM: CBT REDESIGN

Direction
Product: MLF papers with timed tests and guided practice
Character: precise, focused, familiar
World: a computer-based examination desk
Moments: the question-status palette and the result summary carry meaningful colour
Signature: numbered question buttons with separate answered and review states

Audit of the previous interface
- Home hero: decorative orbit artwork and a tilted motivational card obscured the assessment task.
- Headings: italic display type and generic motivational language fitted a marketing page.
- Labels: small uppercase, widely tracked metadata reduced readability.
- Collections: repeated decorative icons and equal cards obscured which papers were available.
- Question navigation: answered and unanswered states omitted review flags and unvisited status.
- Exam interaction: selected and saved answers were not distinguished.
- Figure treatment: a decorative score ring was less direct than marks and counts.
- Spacing and text colour: too many one-off values and near-identical tints.

Nine passes
1. Spacing: 4px rhythm, aligned response controls, consistent padding, and 40px actions.
2. Colour: neutral surfaces, one blue action accent, semantic palette states only.
3. Icons: removed decorative icons; retained only symbols that express an answer state.
4. Copy: assessment names, dates, instructions and direct action labels replace slogans.
5. Type: familiar examination sans serif, readable labels, restrained weights, tabular figures.
6. Layout: full-height question workspace, right-side palette and stationary response actions.
7. Details: clear focus outlines, status labels, a scrollable paper preview, and submission summary.
8. Visuals: the real question palette and score breakdown supply the visual content; no image needed.
9. Motion: surface changes only, no decorative animation, with reduced-motion support.

Behaviour
- Selecting an exam answer creates a draft; Save & Next commits it.
- Palette navigation discards unsaved changes, as explained on screen.
- Mark for Review & Next commits the draft and adds the review flag.
- Answered and marked responses count in grading; unanswered review questions do not.
- Full marks for a completely correct response, zero otherwise; no negative marks.
- Practice choices save immediately and reveal feedback when checked.
- The original question text, options, solutions and 60-minute duration are preserved.

Reference
NTA National Test Abhyas test guidance: https://www.nta.ac.in/Abhyas/test
The interface is an independent MLF practice site; it does not use official agency branding.
